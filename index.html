THIS IS IHAF

Come alone.
Leave with friends.

IHAF — I Have A Friend — is a Kuala Lumpur social community built around one simple idea: meeting new people should feel natural.

We bring strangers together through drinks, sports, games and shared experiences designed to make the first hello easier.
